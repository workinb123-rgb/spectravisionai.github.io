Getting Started

📷 Open the Camera

Allow camera access when prompted, then point your device at the object, scene, or area you want to explore.
🌡️ AI Thermal Simulation

Applies a simulated thermal-style visualization to the camera image to highlight differences in brightness and contrast.
🌌 Infrared Simulation

Applies an infrared-style visual effect to help reveal differences in light and image intensity.
☀️ UV Simulation

Applies a simulated ultraviolet-style visualization effect to the live camera image.
🌙 Low-Light Enhancement

Helps improve the visibility of darker scenes by enhancing the camera image.
🛰️ Satellite Tracking

Use the tracking tools to explore available satellite and location-based information.
🪨 AI Material & Object Recognition

Point the camera at an object or material and use AI analysis to receive identification or informational results when available.
🧭 GPS + AR Exploration

Allow location access to use GPS and augmented-reality exploration features.
⚠️ Important

SpectraVision AI provides simulated visualization and AI-generated analysis for exploration, education, and research. Results may not always be accurate and should not replace professional equipment or expert verification.
