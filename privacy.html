At a Glance

Key point: SpectraVision AI performs its computer-vision analysis on your device. Your live camera frames are processed locally in the app and are not uploaded to any server unless you explicitly capture a photo or video, or choose to save or share content. There is no cloud AI service that receives your live camera stream.

This Privacy Policy describes the data handled by the SpectraVision AI mobile application (the "App") and applies to all users. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address — used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Hashed password — when you register with email/password. Passwords are never stored in plain text; authentication is handled by the App's backend platform.
  • Display name — derived from your email or Google profile, shown next to content you publish.
  • Google profile — if you use Google Sign-In, Google shares your basic profile (name, email, and profile picture URL) with the App for sign-in and display.

Camera data

Live camera frames are accessed for real-time, on-device analysis and are not uploaded. When you choose to capture a photo or record a video, the resulting media file is uploaded to the App's cloud storage and stored with your account (see Cloud Storage & Database).

Audio data

Microphone input is used for the Audio Analyzer (waveform, spectrum, spectrogram) and the Audio Recorder. Audio is processed on-device. Audio recordings you create are kept locally in your browser session and are only stored off-device if you choose to export/download them. The App does not upload audio recordings to its servers.

Location data

GPS location (precise, device-derived latitude/longitude) is accessed only when you enable GPS Tagging in Settings (to embed coordinates in captures) or open the Satellite Tracker (to determine which satellites are overhead). Location is processed on-device; it is stored only when attached to a capture you save, or used transiently for satellite visibility calculations.

Sensor data

  • Magnetometer — magnetic field readings (X/Y/Z, total field, heading) for the Magnetic Sensor panel and anomaly detection. Processed on-device; anomaly events may include a GPS coordinate if location is available.
  • Device orientation / compass — used by the UFO motion-tracking overlay to display heading and orientation. Processed on-device only.

User-generated content & social data

If you use the Community features, the App stores content you publish, including:

  • Discoveries (title, description, photo, category) you upload or share from your Gallery.
  • Comments you write on discoveries.
  • Likes, follows, blocks, and reports you submit (which include your user ID/name and, for reports, the reported content and author).
  • Challenge progress, XP, badges, and achievement statistics tied to your account.

Usage & diagnostics

The App's hosting/backend platform may collect limited, aggregate usage and diagnostic information (such as request logs, app version, and device type) needed to operate, secure, and improve the service. This may include an IP address and standard request metadata processed by the backend.

Local device storage

The App stores preferences on your device using browser storage, including: app settings (theme, resolution, units), panel layout positions, the startup-disclaimer acknowledgment, and (if you opt in) a handle to a local "msvd" gallery folder for saving captures directly to your device.

How We Use Information

  • Provide real-time, on-device multi-spectrum image analysis and visual overlays.
  • Generate simulated thermal, infrared, ultraviolet, and low-light visualizations from visible camera imagery.
  • Provide UFO motion detection and object tracking on the live camera feed.
  • Provide satellite tracking and overhead visibility based on your location.
  • Store, display, and let you manage the captures and content you choose to save or share.
  • Operate community features (feed, comments, likes, follows) and gamification (challenges, XP, badges).
  • Authenticate you, maintain your session, and secure your account.
  • Diagnose faults, prevent abuse, and improve reliability and features.

On-Device AI Processing

All computer-vision analysis in SpectraVision AI runs locally on your device:

  • Material/surface analysis uses on-device pixel heuristics (color ratios, brightness, saturation, edge density) to estimate a material and confidence score. It does not send frames to any server.
  • Thermal, edge, and spectral overlays are rendered on a local canvas from the live video frame.
  • UFO motion tracking uses on-device background subtraction and connected-component detection on a downscaled frame buffer.
  • Estimated temperature and vegetation index are derived from pixel brightness and color ratios on-device and are clearly labeled as AI estimates.

The App does not use a cloud-based AI/LLM service to analyze your camera feed. No live camera frame is transmitted off your device by the analysis features.

Camera & Image Data

Camera access is required for the core camera, analysis, and tracking features. Live frames stay on-device. A photo or video is uploaded to cloud storage only when you press the capture or record button. Each saved capture may include: the processed image/video, an optional unprocessed original (if "Save Original" is on), the active spectral mode, estimated material/confidence/temperature/vegetation index, and optional GPS coordinates (if GPS Tagging is on). You can view, edit, favorite, and delete your captures in the Gallery, and share a capture to the Community.

Microphone & Audio Data

Microphone access is used only while the Audio Analyzer panel is active. The App records audio only when you press Record; recordings are held in your local browser session and are not uploaded. You can export a recording (and its metadata) as a download to your device. Microphone access stops when you leave the Camera tab or close the panel.

Location Data

Location is requested only for GPS Tagging and the Satellite Tracker. The App fetches public satellite position data from a third-party service (see Third-Party Services) and computes which satellites are visible using your on-device GPS — your coordinates are not sent to that satellite service. When GPS Tagging is enabled, coordinates are attached to captures you save. You can disable location at any time in your device settings; satellite tracking will not work without it.

Sensor Data

Magnetometer and device-orientation data are read on-device for the Magnetic Sensor panel and the UFO overlay. Magnetic anomaly events are logged in your local session and may include a GPS coordinate (if available) for your reference; these logs are not uploaded unless you choose to save related content. Sensor availability depends on your device and browser.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your account data is retained while your account is active. You can delete individual captures, discoveries, comments, and other content from within the App at any time.

You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Delete Account. Account deletion is irreversible and removes:

  • Your user record and sign-in account.
  • All your database records: captures, discoveries, comments, likes, reports, blocks, follows, challenge progress, and statistics.
  • All your uploaded media files (photos and videos) from Base44 file storage, including processed captures, saved originals, and shared discovery images.

Deletion begins immediately when you confirm. Once complete, your data cannot be recovered.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, including authenticated access to your own data and row-level isolation so that private records (such as your captures and stats) are only accessible by you. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Permissions: You can deny or revoke Camera, Microphone, Location, and Sensor permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features will not function without them.
  • Content: Delete your captures, discoveries, and comments from within the App.
  • Account: Permanently delete your account and associated database records from Settings.
  • Local data: Clear the App's local storage (settings, layout, disclaimer) by clearing site data for the App.
  • Google: Manage or revoke the App's access to your Google account from your Google account security settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age and is not intended for use by them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can delete it.

Local Storage & Tokens

The App does not use advertising cookies. It uses local browser storage to remember your settings, panel layout, and disclaimer acknowledgment, and an authentication token to keep you signed in. This data stays on your device unless you clear it.

Simulated AI Notice

SpectraVision AI uses computer vision to generate simulated thermal, infrared, ultraviolet, and low-light visualizations from the visible camera image. The App does not provide true thermal imaging or scientific measurement and should not be relied upon as a substitute for specialized equipment. Estimated values (material, temperature, vegetation index) are approximate, on-device estimates.
