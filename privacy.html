Introduction

SpectraVision AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, SpectraVision AI may access:

  • Camera images and live video for real-time AI analysis.
  • Device location (GPS) for augmented reality features, satellite tracking, and environmental awareness.
  • Device orientation and motion sensor data, including the compass, accelerometer, gyroscope, and magnetometer.
  • Device information such as operating system version, device model, and application diagnostics.
  • User account information, including your email address, if you create an account.
  • Photos or screenshots that you choose to save or share.

How We Use Information

  • Provide AI-powered image analysis.
  • Generate AI-simulated thermal, infrared, ultraviolet, and low-light visualizations.
  • Display augmented reality overlays and satellite tracking.
  • Improve application performance and reliability.
  • Diagnose bugs and technical issues.
  • Enhance future features and the overall user experience.

Camera and Sensor Data

Camera images and sensor information are used only to provide real-time functionality within the application. Unless you specifically choose to upload, save, or share content, camera frames are intended to be processed only while the app is in use.

Location Information

Location information is used solely to provide satellite tracking, environmental analysis, mapping, and augmented reality positioning.

You may disable location permissions at any time through your device settings. Some features may no longer function correctly if permissions are disabled.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may only be shared:

  • To protect the security, rights, or safety of users and the application.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Children's Privacy

SpectraVision AI is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
